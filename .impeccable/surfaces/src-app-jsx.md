# ICS home page

Scope: single-page marketing site (React + CSS + motion). Mode: Persuade.
Audience: Delhi business owners / finance and operations heads outsourcing back-office work. Action: call or WhatsApp Kunal Chopra, or send the enquiry form (prefilled WhatsApp message). Proof on hand: name, logo, GSTIN, owner, phone, address only. No stats, clients, testimonials, email or years.
Constraints: logo recreated exactly from letterhead; mobile-first; reduced-motion respected.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The category standard, played straight at Infosys BPM / Wipro craft: a structured corporate services site whose authority comes from order, precision and a direct owner line, not from invented proof. Refuses quirk and fake metrics.

OWN-WORLD: White ground, charcoal ink (#16181D), ICS crimson (#A8132A) reserved for brand, primary action and active state; one deep charcoal band and one crimson CTA band. Thin crimson rule echoing the letterhead. Self-hosted grotesque (Archivo) for all text; logo ICS in a heavy condensed face (Anton). Line icons authored as SVG at one stroke.

STORY: Visitor sees what ICS does (data entry, accounting, back-office) in one line, sees the work being processed in the hero motion graphic, browses nine clearly defined services, understands the four-step process, then calls or WhatsApps the owner.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Utility bar (address, phone). Header: logo left, nav, crimson "Get a quote". Hero left: headline at ~4.5rem, one sentence subcopy, primary "Call Kunal" + WhatsApp. Hero right: animated processing graphic: source documents feed into a live register whose rows type in and tick verified (sample data, labelled).

FORM: Category standard (canon), chosen by user over seed roll; seed key d61f77f2.

Signature interaction: hero processing graphic + services explorer (list + animated detail panel; accordion on mobile). Motion grammar: exponential ease-out, content visible by default, line-draw process rail on scroll.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
