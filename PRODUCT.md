## Users

Businesses that need dependable outsourced back-office work: owners, finance heads and operations managers who want data entry, accounting and backend processing handled accurately without hiring in-house. They evaluate a vendor by clarity of services, trust signals, and how easy it is to get a direct answer from the person in charge.

## Product Purpose

The website introduces ICS (Integrated Care Solutions) as a service provider for back-office operations, lays out its services in a clean, well-defined structure, and turns visitors into enquiries — primarily a phone call or WhatsApp message to the owner. Success: a visitor understands what ICS does within seconds and contacts Kunal Chopra.
